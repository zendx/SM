# Writing for SMPIS

Help someone understand the screen and take the next step after reading a few lines.

- Start with the task: what can the person do here?
- Use familiar words. Say “create a school portal” instead of “provision a tenant workspace”.
- Give short, concrete instructions in the order someone needs them.
- Explain the result when it matters: “Publish the reports so families can view them.”
- Make errors useful: explain what went wrong and what the person can do next. Keep account recovery messages private; never reveal whether an account exists.
- Name buttons after their action. Keep existing names consistent across screens and help text.
- Keep important conditions: payment confirmation, permissions, deadlines, renewal choices and data retention must remain accurate.
- Keep technical delivery details out of school tasks. Explain them in owner settings or technical documentation when needed.
- Use a calm, respectful tone. Avoid slogans in instructions, unnecessary jargon and messages that blame the reader.

Before publishing, ask: Can someone unfamiliar with this screen understand what it is for, what to do and what happens next?
