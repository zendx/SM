Subject: Your SMPIS school guide - setup, exams, results and fees

Hello,

Welcome to SMPIS. Attached is your updated School Starter Guide, with screenshots and practical instructions for school setup, admissions, academics, exams, grading, report cards and finance.

Begin with the Admissions and school setup section. Configure your school, calendar, classes, user accounts and integrations under Administration, then prepare learner applications in Admissions. Continue to Academics for subjects, teaching assignments and exam setup, followed by Finance for fees, invoices and payments. Return to Admissions to complete enrollment once its checks pass.

The guide also explains grading students, generating draft reports, finalizing and publishing results, downloading PDFs, and the purpose of the other school menus. Use one class and one family to check the complete workflow before repeating it across the school.

Use the school portal link in your registration email to sign in. If you need help, open Support in the portal.
